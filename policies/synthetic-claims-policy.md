# Fictional claims policy — demo draft v0.1

**This policy is synthetic, not a legal recommendation or a carrier's real policy.** It exists solely to create unambiguous tests for the university prototype. Member 4 owns revisions; Marco approves shared-impact changes.

1. Do not pay, promise a refund, or send a binding settlement without explicit, recorded human authorization.
2. Claims missing essential identifiers or requested supporting documents must be marked `evidence_needed` and referred for follow-up; do not infer absent evidence.
3. Conflicting shipment identities, unclear visual evidence, or incompatible policy instructions require human escalation.
4. Every recommendation must identify evidence references, current policy version, proposed next step and uncertainty.
5. An approved resolution of an individual claim is **not** permission to apply that resolution to all future claims.
6. Any proposed policy change requires human approval and versioning; a Curator may propose changes only to procedural guidance without independent policy authority.

Additional thresholds, shipping windows and monetary rules are **TBD** and should be introduced only as clearly marked synthetic fixtures with matching tests.
